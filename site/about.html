About MFS

The spaces we depend on matter.

Every property is an ecosystem of people, assets, services and spaces. Since 2015, MFS has brought them together through professional facilities management.

Explore our story ↓

01Our story

Behind every successful property is a team that keeps it working.

Offices, estates, schools, shopping centres and homes shape how people live and work. The services that keep them clean, safe and well maintained often work behind the scenes.

Since 2015, Moisapula Facilities Solutions has provided those services with care and professionalism, through our own trained teams and, for building and civil works, our partner Ecotech Engineering.

  1. 2015FoundedMoisapula Facilities Solutions is registered in Gauteng.
  2. GrowthMore servicesCleaning, grounds, property care and support services, delivered by our own teams.
  3. PartnershipEcotech EngineeringBuilding and civil works delivered with our strategic partner.
  4. TodayLevel 1 B-BBEEServing homes, estates and businesses across Gauteng.
