What we do

Cleaning & Hygiene services

Daily, scheduled and once-off cleaning, delivered by trained, uniformed teams with the right equipment and consumables.

General office cleaning

Daily and scheduled cleaning of workspaces, kitchens and boardrooms.

Estate and common area cleaning

Entrances, clubhouses, walkways and shared facilities.

Home cleaning

Regular, spring and move-in or move-out cleans for private homes.

Deep cleaning

Periodic intensive cleans, including carpets and hard floors.

Hygiene and washroom services

Sanitiser, hygiene units and sanitary bin servicing.

Consumable supply management

Toilet paper, hand towels and soap, monitored and replenished.

Window and glass cleaning

Interior and exterior glazing and shopfronts.

High-level cleaning

Ceilings, light fittings and high surfaces, using safe work-at-height methods.

Post-construction cleaning

Builders' cleans that get new or renovated spaces ready for handover.

Pressure washing

Paving, walls, parking areas and bin areas.

Safety and quality

Standards on every job

  • Colour-coded cloths and mops to prevent cross-contamination
  • Safety data sheets for every chemical on site
  • Safe work-at-height methods for high-level cleaning
  • Supervisor inspections against an agreed checklist
